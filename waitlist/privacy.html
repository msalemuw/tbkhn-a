Privacy notice

Last updated 10 October 2026

tabkheen A is a neighbourhood home-cooking app in Egypt: people cook, neighbours reserve a plate and pick it up. This notice covers the waitlist on this website, before the app opens.

What we collect

  • Your name and WhatsApp number.
  • Whether you want to order, cook or both, and your governorate and community.
  • Which ad or invite link brought you here, and the invite code we give you.

Why

  • To message you on WhatsApp when the app opens. We send launch news only, no other marketing.
  • To decide which areas and communities open first.
  • To count which ads and invites work. If you came from a Facebook or Instagram ad, Meta may count that you joined; we never give Meta your name or number.

Who can see it

Only the tabkheen A team. It is stored with our database provider, Supabase. We do not sell it or share it with cooks, advertisers or anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until six months after the app opens, then we delete it. If you sign up in the app, your account is covered by the app’s own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your details at any time, or reply STOP to any of our WhatsApp messages. Write to tbkheen.A@gmail.com.
